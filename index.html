How it works — BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)

  1. Get a free Google Gemini API key from aistudio.google.com/apikey (takes 1 minute).
  2. Open any tool below and paste your key in the key box. It is saved only in your browser.
  3. Use the tool. Requests go straight from your browser to the AI provider — nothing passes through us.

OpenRouter and Groq keys also work — just switch the provider inside any tool.
